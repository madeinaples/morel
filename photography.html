Another way of telling

Here, photography is not decoration. An image can remain simply an image, or it can open a memory, a place, a sensation and sometimes a story.

The photographs may be revisited, but the subject and intention of the original shot remain intact. Beneath each image there will be a place and year, a short interpretation and, when there is a natural connection, a link to the article that continues the story.

Photography

Original images, taken before I knew they might one day become part of a story.

Another way of telling

Every photograph in this archive is an original image taken by Andrea Morel over the years. These are not stock images or photographs collected from the web: they belong to his personal archive.

Here, photography is not decoration. An image can remain simply an image, or it can open a memory, a place, a sensation and sometimes a story.

The photographs may be revisited, but the subject and intention of the original shot remain intact. Beneath each image there is a place and year, a short interpretation and, when there is a natural connection, a link to the article that continues the story.

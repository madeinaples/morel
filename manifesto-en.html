MOREL · The way we look

Our perspective

Starting from one life to speak about life. Without turning it into a lesson.

MOREL starts from lives because that is where questions become real.

A story can belong to one person and, at the same time, touch many others.

That is the point that interests us: not explaining who someone is, but understanding what that life sets in motion inside us.

That is why the personal and the collective are never truly separate here.

Never tell someone’s story without asking where it meets your own. And never tell your own story without leaving a door open for others.

We are not looking for perfect people.

We are interested in contradictions, changes of direction, and the things that refuse to fit neatly inside a definition.

The right to change your mind. To leave a dream without calling it failure. To refuse to turn the body, success, a relationship or an identity into a résumé.

A story can be deep without becoming heavy.

It can use irony without becoming cynical.

It can scratch without attacking.

We are not interested in telling the reader what to think. We are interested in leaving something in front of them long enough for one question to appear:

where does this meet me?

MOREL changes together with the people it tells and the person writing it.

It is not a promise of consistency.

It is a way of looking.

MOREL

Depth without heaviness.
Irony without cynicism.

Artificial intelligence use notice

How AI tools are used within the Andrea Morel project.

Andrea Morel is an editorial project under human responsibility. Artificial intelligence tools may be used to assist with writing, revision, translation, visual design, and the creation or editing of images.

Published texts are selected, edited and approved through a human editorial review process. Final responsibility for the content remains with the editorial project.

Some images on this website have been created or edited with artificial intelligence systems for artistic, narrative or illustrative purposes. Unless otherwise stated, they should not be understood as documentary photographs of real people or events.

Where artificially generated or manipulated content could reasonably be mistaken for an authentic representation of a real person, place or event, an additional disclosure will be provided beside the content or on the relevant page.

This notice is intended to provide transparency without interfering with the reading experience or the creative nature of the project.

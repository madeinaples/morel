Why this exists

Reality, told
with enough distance.

This site is a place for experiences that are easier to tell honestly when the private person behind them can remain private. Andrea Morel is the name on the page; the life, observations and emotional material come from a real person.

The editorial promise.
Memory can be subjective. Privacy sometimes requires changing a name, place or identifying detail. What is not manufactured is the lived core of the work.

Take your time. Read a story, listen when there is something worth hearing, pause over a photograph. There is no right way through this notebook.

About the nameA

A real voice · A chosen name

Privacy without
pretending.

Andrea Morel is a pen name. It separates the private person from the public work and creates enough distance to write openly.

The pseudonym is not intended to impersonate a fictional individual. The site makes the arrangement explicit so the reader never has to wonder what is being hidden and why.

The work matters more than a résumé. The reader is invited to know the voice through what it notices, remembers, questions and chooses to tell.

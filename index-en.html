About the nameA

A real voice · A chosen name

Privacy without
pretending.

Andrea Morel is a pen name. It separates the private person from the public work and creates enough distance to write openly.

Memory can be subjective and identifying details may be changed for privacy. The lived core of the work is not manufactured.

The reader is not asked to follow a character. The invitation is to recognise, disagree, remember and change their mind.

The signatureC

Cristiano Maiello

I'm not finished.
I changed my mind.

For a while I signed my work as Andrea Morel. It wasn't a lie. It was a way to begin.

Now I want to take ownership of my ideas, without a mask between me and what I create. I have nothing left to prove, but plenty left to discover.

I like to play, take wrong turns and try things I don't yet know how to do. I want to keep the curious child within me from being silenced by the expectations of adulthood.

I haven't finished playing. And I don't see why I should.

A promise to the reader

A story needn't explain everything. It should offer enough for each reader to connect their own dots.

Contradictions interest me more than certainties. Irony does not exclude depth. Curiosity has no age limit.

Here you'll find stories, photography, music and doors that open when you least expect them.

The editorial promise. A story may begin with a real person, famous or unknown. If it remains only about that person, it is not enough. It has to open a space for the reader.
Andrea Morel

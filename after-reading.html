Spaghetti with garlic, olive oil and chilli

The Neapolitan dinner that saves an evening without asking questions.

Stories make you think. Then, eventually, you also have to eat.

Spaghetti with garlic, olive oil and chilli is one of those dishes that looks easy only to people who have never ruined it. There are very few ingredients. That is precisely the problem: there is nowhere to hide.

For two people

200 grams of spaghetti, 2 garlic cloves, extra virgin olive oil, chilli, fresh parsley, salt and a little pasta water.

How to make it

Bring the water to the boil and salt it. Meanwhile, pour a generous amount of olive oil into a pan and add thinly sliced garlic and chilli.

Warm it slowly. The garlic should turn golden, not brown. If it turns brown, you have organised a small Mediterranean funeral and it is best to start again.

Drain the spaghetti very al dente, keeping a cup of the cooking water. Transfer it to the pan and toss, adding a little water at a time until the oil and starch form a light sauce.

Turn off the heat, add chopped parsley and toss once more. Serve immediately. This pasta waits for nobody, not even an interesting man.

In the glass

I would open a chilled Fiano di Avellino, but not an icy one. It has enough personality to sit at the table without turning dinner into a wine seminar.

If the conversation works, you will have made an excellent impression. If it does not, at least you will have eaten well.

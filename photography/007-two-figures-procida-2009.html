Two figures

Not everything that happens between two people needs to be explained.

← Back to all photographs
Interpretation

I do not remember what they were saying to each other.

Perhaps that is exactly what still interests me about the photograph.

Two figures, no faces, the sea behind them, and enough space for the viewer to imagine the rest.

Sometimes a photograph works better when it confirms nothing.

Andrea Morel

Laundry

Sometimes a city tells its story without showing a face.

Interpretation

In the dark, the laundry almost stops being an everyday object.

It becomes presence, colour, small traces of lives we cannot see.

A city can also tell its story through what it leaves suspended above our heads.

Andrea Morel

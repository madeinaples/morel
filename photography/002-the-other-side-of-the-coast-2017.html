The other side of the coast

Looking towards the horizon without ever quite giving up the thought that one day you might begin again.

← Back to all photographs
Interpretation

Solitude can also mean looking towards the horizon, because we never quite stop dreaming that one day we might begin again.

Who knows, perhaps on the other side of the coast.

Because it is true: changing your address does not solve everything.

But it changes the perspective.

Andrea Morel

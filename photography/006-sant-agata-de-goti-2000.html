Sant'Agata at night

Some places seem to remember even when we have stopped doing so.

← Back to all photographs
Interpretation

Night removes detail and lets form emerge.

The houses seem to cling to the stone while the light draws only what is needed.

Perhaps some places do not grow old: they accumulate memory.

Andrea Morel

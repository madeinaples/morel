Where the light ends

Light does not erase the shadow. The shadow is what allows it to exist.

← Back to all photographs
Interpretation

There are images in which light is not there to reveal everything.

Here it almost does the opposite: it dazzles, erases detail and leaves the palms in shadow. In the distance, the water remains barely visible, like a quiet line.

Sun, water, contrast and stillness coexist in the same moment.

Perhaps it is the contrast itself that makes the scene feel silent: light does not erase the shadow. The shadow is what allows it to exist.

Andrea Morel

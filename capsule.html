CAPRI

Warm wood. Turquoise. Stone.
Late afternoon, somewhere in Capri.

For me, elegance is not about showing off. It is not the label and it is not the price. It is knowing how to put things together, even things of little value, and choosing the small details that make the difference.

Capri, with its warm wood, turquoise and stone, I wear with a simple white linen shirt, left natural and freshly dried, without chasing the perfection of an iron.

Cream linen trousers.
Leather sandals.

Skin still warm from the sun.

One spray of Hermès Vetiver. Not a shower.

The rest is unnecessary.

Andrea Morel

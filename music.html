Stories find a new voice.

Andrea Morel brings the stories, contradictions and encounters that shape his world into music.

His songs explore desire, ambiguous friendships, belonging and words spoken too easily. What happens between two people, and what changes when someone leaves the room.

Across pop, electronic music, funk and house, intimacy meets rhythm, while irony lets something more uncomfortable surface. Stories to listen to, to dance to, to recognise yourself in.

Section 03 · Entrusted lives

Entrusted Lives

A life can be someone else’s and still open a question that belongs to all of us.

These pages begin with encounters, confidences and lives other than my own.

When a story grows from real events, names, places and identifying details may be changed for privacy. The aim is not to own someone else’s experience, but to protect what is human inside it.

I do not pretend to disappear from the frame. The editorial question is always the same: where does this person’s story meet something in us?

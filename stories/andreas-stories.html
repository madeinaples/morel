Stories

A life is the starting point. Not the boundary of the story.

These pages begin with an experience, an encounter, a city, a confidence, a desire or something that keeps leaving a trace.

Some lives are mine. Others have been entrusted to me, or I have encountered them closely enough not to forget them. That difference changes the responsibility of the telling, not the editorial room where the story belongs.

This is not an orderly diary, and it is not a collection of human “cases”. A story works only when the life it begins with opens something wider for the reader.

Start from a life without stopping at that life: that is the rule.

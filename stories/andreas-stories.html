Section 01 · Lived experience

Stories

Start from a life without stopping at that life.

These pages begin with lived experience: memory, relationships, solitude, desire, the body, age and change.

They are not an orderly diary and they are not here to prove that one life is more interesting than another. The personal detail matters only when it can open something wider.

The voice may be first person, but the destination is never meant to be only the writer.

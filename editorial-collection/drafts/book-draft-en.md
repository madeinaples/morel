## Draft review questions

When reading the complete draft, we will test:

- whether the voice remains consistent;
- which themes are repeated;
- where transitions are needed;
- which articles must become full chapters;
- whether the Vinarium is the narrative centre or a separate section;
- what promise the book makes to the reader and whether it keeps that promise.

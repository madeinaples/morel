CRISTIANO MAIELLO

About
me

No mask. One voice, many ideas.

I'm not here to prove that life can begin again at a certain age. I'm here because I haven't finished exploring.

IN MY OWN NAME

I'm not finished.
I changed my mind.

For a while I signed my work as Andrea Morel. The words were already mine. Now I want my own name on the cover.

01 · THE SIGNATURE

The ideas
are mine.

My name is Cristiano Maiello. Andrea Morel was a pen name, not a fabricated person. Today I use my own name because I want to take ownership of what I write, photograph and create.

I have nothing to prove. But I still have things to say.

02 · PLAY

I haven't
finished playing.

I like risking an awkward moment, changing direction and trying something I don't yet know how to do. I don't want to be a child again; I want to keep adulthood from silencing my curiosity.

If a door is closed, I'd at least like to check whether it has a handle.

03 · A POINT OF VIEW

Not a promise
of consistency.

I'm interested in contradictions, the right to change your mind and dreams you can leave behind without calling it failure.

A story can be deep without becoming heavy. It can use irony without turning cynical.

04 · THREADS

Thoughts don't
stay alone.

Threads aren't neat shelves. They connect stories, photographs, sounds and ideas.

A story doesn't always end at the bottom of the page. Sometimes it opens another door.

05 · PHOTOGRAPHY

Images
open doors.

I photograph places, details and traces. Not to illustrate my writing, but to see what words cannot yet say.

A photograph might be the beginning of a story.

View photography ↗

06 · MUSIC & POP CULTURE

Listening
is observing.

Jazz, funk and pop culture aren't only a soundtrack. They're different ways of reading the present and its contradictions.

MOREL remains the name of my existing music catalogue; Cristiano Maiello signs the new jazz chapter.

Explore music ↗

07 · INTIMACY

Mystery, yes.
Vagueness, no.

I write about desire, encounters, identity and memory. Not everything must be explained, but a reader deserves enough clues to connect their own dots.

Intimacy can be told without becoming an exhibition.

08 · TWO LANGUAGES

Two languages.
One point of view.

I write in Italian and English. The rhythm may change, but the questions remain.

I'm not looking for a definitive answer. I prefer a question that stays.
